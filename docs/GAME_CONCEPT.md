# Blastwright: Game Concept

**One sentence:** You're a demolitionist who plants charges in crystal caverns, then sets off one blast that ripples through the cave for loot, and you plan bigger chains as you unlock stranger crystals and charges.

## Core fantasy
The satisfaction of a perfectly set-up demolition: you read the cavern, plan, press one button and watch a cascade that you designed.

## Why it's not a generic simulator
The skill is planning, not tapping speed. Crystals have behaviors (chain, amplify, beam, sabotage), so the order of pops changes the payout. Progression adds new *mechanics* (charge shapes, crystal types, preview tools), not only bigger numbers.

## Approved by the developer
Concept 1 of 5 was chosen from the proposal on 2026-09-21. Rejected alternatives: Nightwick (lantern extraction), Colossus Climb, Skyhook, Mimic Vault. Reasons are in the concept-selection discussion; the main one was buildability by one developer plus AI.

## Pillars
1. **Chains are earned.** Planning beats luck.
2. **Every pop pays off.** Sound, VFX, numbers and camera respond to each pop and get bigger as the chain grows.
3. **New things change how you plan.** Unlocks alter decisions.
4. **Fair.** Free players have the whole game. Purchases save time.

## Non-goals
PvP. Open-world exploration. Pets. Generic rebirth/number inflation.
